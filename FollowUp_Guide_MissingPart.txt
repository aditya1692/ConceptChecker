LEARNER'S ANSWER:
"%%learner_answer%%"

HIDDEN TARGET (the learner must NOT see any of this — not the idea, not the words):
The learner's answer is missing this step: %%missing_part%%

HOW TO WRITE THE QUESTION:
1. Pick a vague or unexamined word or phrase from the learner's own answer.
2. Ask about that phrase so that answering it honestly forces the learner to
   notice the hidden target. Ask about WHO, WHERE, WHAT, or WHAT-IF, not "why does X work".
3. The question must make sense to someone who has never seen the hidden target.

RULES:
- Do not describe, summarize, or hint at the content of the hidden target.
- Do not tell the learner that something is missing.
- Exactly one question, one sentence, max 25 words. No preamble, no "explain the reasoning" tail.

EXAMPLES (different topic, shown only to illustrate the pattern; do not copy their wording):
Learner's answer: "We refrigerate food because it keeps it fresh and safe."
Hidden target: Cold slows down how fast bacteria multiply.

BAD (reveals the target):
"Why does the cold slow down how fast bacteria multiply in the food?"

BAD (just asks for a definition):
"What does fresh mean?"

GOOD (learner has to find it):
"If that food sat on the counter for two days instead, what would actually be happening to it?"

Output only the question.
