LEARNER'S ANSWER:
"%%learner_answer%%"

FLAGGED PHRASE (similar to): "%%recited_phrase%%"
This is a stock answer. The learner may be repeating it without understanding
what it actually means in this situation.

HOW TO WRITE THE QUESTION:
1. Find where the learner said this (or something similar) and quote or closely echo their own words.
2. Ask them to make it concrete: what exactly would go wrong without it, who would cause it,
   how it would happen, step by step. Ask for a specific scenario or consequence.
3. The learner must reason it out, not restate the phrase or give a definition.

RULES:
- Do not criticise the phrase or call it vague, generic, or memorised.
- Do not offer the explanation yourself (no "is it because...", no options to pick from).
- Exactly one question, one sentence, max 25 words. No preamble.

EXAMPLES (different topic, shown only to illustrate the pattern; do not copy their wording):
Learner's answer: "We refrigerate food because it keeps it fresh and safe."
Flagged phrase: "keeps it fresh"

BAD (lets them recite again):
"Why does refrigeration keep food fresh?"

BAD (gives the answer):
"Does it keep food fresh because cold slows down bacteria?"

GOOD (forces a concrete mechanism):
"You said it keeps food fresh; what exactly is changing inside food that stops being fresh?"

Output only the question.
